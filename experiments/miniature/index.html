The line for the chosen time: stand anywhere on it and the sun is behind part of the tower. The ring is where it is behind the sphere.
The same line at each hour, with its time at the end or the edge of the view.
The moon behind the antenna: it still shows round the thin mast.
The day’s path of sphere spots; dashed where a building is in the way.
A street henge: sunlight or moonlight runs down the street now.

Line up the sun and moon with Berlin

Find where to stand so the sun or moon sits behind the TV tower, or shines straight down a street.

  1. Pick a day and a time. The coloured bars above the slider show when something lines up. Open the panel for the day’s list.
  2. Read the map. Tower alignments: stand on a line and the sun or moon is behind the tower at that hour. Street henges: glowing streets line up with the sun or moon, like Manhattanhenge.
  3. See the shot. Drag the photographer onto the map, or hover a line and choose “View from here”. Walk, change lenses, or take off as a drone.
