## What Round 4 must fix
1. RAIN. The clay teardrops are too large and say nothing about intensity. Design rain that reads as TYPE and INTENSITY: drizzle vs steady rain vs shower vs downpour. The data per hour is probability (0–100) and mm (depth). Think: density, drop size, clustering, duration continuity across hours — showers punctuate, fronts persist. Keep it clay-style (soft, glossy, tactile) but SMALL. Snow is not in the data; ignore it.
2. MOON WINDOWS. Frames must exist for the moon too. A moon window = night hour where the moon is up, sufficiently illuminated (phase), and the sky is clear enough. New moon → NO window at all (there is no moon in the sky — this is a hard rule the user stated). Moon frames must be visually distinct from sun frames but siblings of the same idea (think: sun frames warm/green-gold, moon frames cool/silver-blue). The shared phase axis at the bottom must also carry moonrise/moonset or moonlight information somehow — the axis is the once-per-card place for what every day shares, but moon timing differs per day, so decide where moon up-time lives (per-day row vs axis).
